I am an undergraduate student in **Intelligence Science and Technology** at [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn/) (class of 2029).

My research interests center on LLM agents:

- **Agent harnesses** — tools, MCP, context management, memory, and skills;
- **Recursive self-improvement (RSI)** — agents that improve their own skills, prompts, or training signals under reliable evaluation;
- **Agent RL** — post-training with verifiable rewards (RLVR), reward models, and verifiers.

Recently I have been reproducing self-improvement methods at small scale and paying close attention to whether the evaluation can be trusted: [recuris-lite](https://github.com/VioletVenti/recuris-lite) evolves an agent's skill memory while the weights stay frozen, and [cure-lite](https://github.com/VioletVenti/cure-lite) co-evolves a coder and a unit tester with RL. Both use [Klaude Code](https://github.com/VioletVenti/Klaude-Code), a Claude Code-style coding agent I built from scratch.
